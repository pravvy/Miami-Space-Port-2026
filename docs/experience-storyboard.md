# Miami Space Port — Six-Scene Concept Storyboard

A short visual narrative introducing the project from regional vision through mission execution.

1. **The vision:** sunrise over the Miami coastline; title reveal, “Dream. Build. Launch.”
2. **The port:** aerial establishing view of a proposed future-facing spaceport campus and connected infrastructure.
3. **The people:** engineers, operators, researchers, and students collaborating across the mission ecosystem.
4. **Mission control:** telemetry walls, clear operator roles, and a shared operational picture.
5. **Launch to orbit:** vehicle departure and transition to orbital operations, presented as a conceptual visualization.
6. **The future:** orbital infrastructure and Earth horizon; close on the Miami Space Port identity and invitation to build responsibly.

## Tone and visual direction

Use a hybrid visual language: cinematic, aspirational establishing shots balanced with credible technical interfaces, legible data, and grounded human activity. Favor deep space blues, restrained cyan highlights, warm coastal light, and clear typography.

## Production note

This storyboard is a creative concept, not a depiction of an approved launch site, scheduled mission, or operational capability.
